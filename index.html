Frequently Asked Questions

How do I buy a title?

Top up your wallet from the Profile tab (or when prompted at purchase), then tap Buy on any title. The cost is deducted from your wallet balance.

How do I publish my work?

Go to the Publish tab and walk the 8-step wizard: Format → Details → AI Disclosure → Content → Cover → Attestation → Pricing → Review. Submit to the AI Editor and, if it passes the 85% commercial-quality bar, publish to the marketplace.

How do payouts work?

Creators keep 85% of net proceeds (after Apple's commission). Payouts are processed through Stripe Connect Express. Connect a bank account from Profile → Set up payouts.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Account → Delete Account. This wipes your on-device data and, where applicable, closes your Stripe Connect account.
