AI Marketplace — Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 May 2026

Summary

We collect only what we need to run your account, process purchases, and show creators how their titles perform. Your data is encrypted on device, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Account details you provide (publisher name, email)
  • Your purchases and library
  • Titles you submit and their AI-disclosure metadata
  • Basic, aggregated usage needed to power the Top 10 and Trending charts

AI disclosure data

For every title you publish you tell us which AI systems produced it. This disclosure is shown publicly on the title page so buyers know what they are getting.

How data is protected

Sensitive data stored on your device — your identity, entitlements and drafts — is encrypted at rest using AES-GCM. The encryption key is generated on first launch and held in the device Keychain; it is never bundled with the app or sent to us.

Payments & third parties

Buyer purchases are processed through Apple In-App Purchase — we receive confirmation of a completed transaction, never your card number. Creator payouts are processed by Stripe (Stripe Connect Express); Stripe collects identification details required by financial regulation. Our backend runs on Cloudflare Workers. Each of these providers has its own privacy policy.

Data linked to your identity

  • Email address — for account creation and notifications
  • Name — your publisher / pen name
  • Purchase history — to manage your library and wallet balance

None of this data is used for tracking or advertising.

Data not linked to your identity

  • Aggregated catalog and chart analytics
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

Your choices & account deletion

Delete your account from Profile → Account → Delete Account. This wipes your on-device encrypted store and, where applicable, asks our backend to close your Stripe Connect account. Anonymised aggregates and records we must retain by law (for example tax records of completed purchases) are kept.

Children

AI Marketplace is not directed to children under 13. Titles carry a maturity rating set by the creator.
