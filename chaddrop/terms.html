What ChadDrop Is

ChadDrop analyzes text messages for psychological patterns and provides humorous, research-informed interpretations. It is designed for entertainment and self-reflection.

ChadDrop is not:

  • Therapy, counseling, or mental health treatment

Your Content

You paste text into ChadDrop for analysis. In offline mode, this text stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. If AI proxy mode is enabled, your text is sent only to generate the decode result and is not stored by the proxy.

Do not paste text that identifies or endangers another person. You are responsible for the content you input.

Safety

If a message contains threat, harm, or intimidation language, ChadDrop will flag it and direct you to safety resources. If you feel unsafe, contact someone you trust or local emergency services immediately. Do not rely on this app in a crisis.

Using ChadDrop does not create a therapist-client, attorney-client, or any other professional relationship. The interpretations, psychology notes, and suggested replies are for entertainment — use your own judgment.
