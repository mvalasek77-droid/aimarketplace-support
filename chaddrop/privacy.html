ChadDrop — Privacy Policy

Last updated June 11, 2026

Summary

ChadDrop does not collect personal data in its default offline mode. Your pasted messages stay on your device. We never sell your data, and we never track you.

Default: Offline Mode

ChadDrop works entirely on your device by default. When you paste a text, it is analyzed by the built-in pattern engine. No data leaves your phone.

  • No account required
  • No data collection
  • No tracking
  • No third-party advertising

If AI Proxy Is Enabled

If the app is configured with an AI proxy URL, pasted text is sent to that proxy only to generate the decode result. The proxy does not log raw messages, does not store them, and does not link them to any identity.

  • Pasted text is processed solely for App Functionality
  • The proxy should not log, store, or share raw user messages
  • No data is linked to your identity

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No account or registration data
  • No email address or personal identifiers
  • No location data
  • No browsing history
  • No analytics or crash reporting
  • No advertising identifiers

Third Parties

ChadDrop does not use any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, or tracking. The optional AI proxy communicates with a server you configure — that server's privacy practices are governed by its own privacy policy.

Children

ChadDrop is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data Retention

In offline mode, no data is retained beyond your current session. Pasted text exists only in the app's memory while you use it and is cleared when you close the app or tap Clear. If AI proxy mode is enabled, the proxy should not retain your text after returning the result.

Your Rights

Because ChadDrop does not collect personal data in offline mode, there is no data to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions about data processed by an AI proxy you configured, contact the proxy operator.

Privacy Manifest

The app's privacy manifest declares no required-reason API usage and no tracking domains.
