What is ChadDrop?

ChadDrop is an entertainment app that decodes confusing text messages using psychology research, dating dynamics, and a healthy dose of humor. Paste a text, get the truth.

It is not therapy, legal advice, or a safety service. Think of it as your brutally honest bestie who reads between the lines.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does ChadDrop work?

Paste a text message into ChadDrop. The app scans for psychological patterns — future faking, love bombing, gaslighting, convenience bids, and more — then gives you a plain-English translation, the psychology behind it, questions to ask yourself, and suggested replies.

Is ChadDrop accurate?

ChadDrop is for entertainment and reflection. It uses research-backed patterns (Gottman, Buss, Rusbult) but it's reading text, not mind-reading. Trust your gut and use the confirmation questions to cross-check.

Does ChadDrop store my messages?

No. In default offline mode, everything stays on your device. If AI proxy mode is enabled, your text is sent only to generate the decode result — it is not logged or stored on the server.

What if a message feels threatening?

If a message contains threat or harm language, ChadDrop will flag it and direct you to safety resources. If you feel unsafe, skip the app and contact someone you trust or local emergency services.
